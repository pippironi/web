The Count of Monte Cristo
Really really enjoyed this one. I had been meaning to read it for a long while, but the length was putting me off. Someone told me that I would be so immersed in the story I wouldn't notice the length. They were right. Such a well crafted story, never gets dull. The buildup to the events in the end is absolutely delightful to follow. Definitely go and grab a copy if you've ever thought of reading it!
